# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The full text is available at the link above. By participating in this project, you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers at `security@testlookup.app`. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.
