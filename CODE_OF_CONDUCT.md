# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The full text is available at the link above. By participating in this project
— in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space —
you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Code of Conduct reports go to the project maintainers (listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)), not to the security channel:

1. Open a new issue and choose **Code of Conduct report**. The form asks only
   for a link to where it happened and how a maintainer can contact you
   privately — **do not put sensitive details in the issue itself**, because
   issues are public. A maintainer will contact you, continue privately, and
   hide or lock the issue.
2. If the concern is about a maintainer, say so in the form without naming
   details; another maintainer handles it, and the person concerned takes no
   part in the decision.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the
reporter of an incident. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
community impact guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent
ban).
