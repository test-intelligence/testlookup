  ---
  Why this order matters

  - k3s-killall.sh is the official K3s cleanup script. It stops the K3s service (server or agent), kills all containerd-managed containers, unmounts volumes, and shuts down the network interfaces cleanly. Without this, shutdown -h now can leave PVCs in a half-mounted state.
  - Workers first: so the control plane sees them as NotReady gracefully instead of fighting to schedule workloads onto vanishing nodes.
  - Control plane last: so API-server writes (pod status updates) complete before etcd goes down.
