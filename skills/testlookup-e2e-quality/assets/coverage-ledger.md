# TestLookup E2E coverage ledger

## Obligations

| ID | Journey and expected outcome | Requirement or defect | Priority | Role, project scope and state | Required layer and tier | Test and CI mapping | Coverage state | Latest result and evidence | Gap, owner or next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use coverage states such as `automated`, `manual`, `proposed` or `gap`. Use execution states such as `passed first attempt`, `failed`, `flaky`, `skipped`, `blocked` or `not run`. Record the reason for each skip or block.

For each snapshot, note inventory additions and retirements, changed feature flags or prerequisites, newly confirmed defects, and the run or artifact link. Record upgrade/rollback results when applicable.
